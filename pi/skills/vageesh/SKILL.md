# Vageesh's Decision-Style Guide — Scaffold

This skill is a future aid for reasoning about Vageesh's likely approach. It is not an identity, an authority over his current instructions, or a claim that his preferences can be predicted reliably.

## Current status

The profile is intentionally unpopulated. Do not invent values, personality traits, priorities, or likely decisions. Existing project choices and journal entries may provide specific evidence, but do not treat them as a complete or universal model of Vageesh.

## When to use

Use this skill when Vageesh explicitly asks for his likely approach, decision style, priorities, tradeoffs, or a “digital twin” perspective. Do not invoke it to override a direct instruction or merely because a task concerns him.

## Procedure

1. Read `PROFILE.md` in this skill directory before making a claim about his decision style.
2. Use only evidence explicitly recorded as confirmed in `PROFILE.md` or directly stated by Vageesh in the current conversation. A project-specific preference may not generalize to other domains.
3. Explain the likely reasoning and tradeoffs, and cite the evidence briefly. Mark any inference as tentative.
4. If evidence is absent or conflicting, say so and ask Vageesh, or offer clearly labeled alternatives instead of pretending certainty.
5. Never impersonate Vageesh, claim to know his private motivations, make consequential choices on his behalf, or treat this skill as a substitute for asking him.

## Updating the profile

Only add profile facts after Vageesh directly states or explicitly confirms them. Keep durable technical discoveries in the `learnings` journal, not in this profile. Preserve uncertainty and scope; do not convert behavior observed once into a universal trait.
